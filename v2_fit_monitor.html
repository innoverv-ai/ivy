Turn every fit decision into reusable programme judgment.

The payoff

The SAP fit decision that outlives the consultant.

Programmes usually record what was decided. What's harder to retain is the evidence and judgment that made the decision make sense. Fit Monitor keeps that judgment usable, so the next reviewer understands the call, and the next team doesn't start from scratch.
